Mykori Dessert Cafe

Mykori Dessert Cafe is the 21st century version of the traditional Japanese Dessert House in Malaysia. Since opening our first outlet in Bandar Puchong Jaya in 2016, Mykori has grown to become the market leader in the premium shaved ice dessert sector in Malaysia — built on a no-compromise approach to ingredients, presentation, and service.
